## Memory

`peak` is the process `phys_footprint` - what Activity Monitor calls Memory.
`weights` is the footprint growth across `load()`. The `mlx` columns are
MLX's own accounting: `mlx peak` is total MLX memory at its highest during
the timed runs, **weights included** - resetting the peak counter zeroes the
high-water mark but frees nothing, so the next allocation restores it.
`act.` is that minus the resident weights: an estimate of the activations,
not a measurement, and `-` where the provider had not materialised its
weights by the end of `load()` (see Notes).

| case | peak | weights | mlx weights | mlx peak | mlx act. | mlx cache | cpu util |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| `mlx.mossformer2_se_48k.fp32` | 884.9 MiB | 12.7 MiB | lazy | 821.2 MiB | - | 464.2 MiB | 0.33 |
| `mlx.mossformer2_se_48k.fp16` | 887.7 MiB | 12.3 MiB | lazy | 804.5 MiB | - | 490.0 MiB | 0.39 |

## Notes

- `mlx.mossformer2_se_48k.fp32`, `mlx.mossformer2_se_48k.fp16`, `mlx.mossformer2_se_48k.int8`, `mlx.mossformer2_se_48k.int6`, `mlx.mossformer2_se_48k.int4` had not materialised weights when `load()` returned - MLX arrays are lazy and these providers do not `eval` at load. Their `load` column excludes weight materialisation and their `first` column includes it, so those two columns are not the same split of work as the other rows'. Total and RTF are unaffected.
- `mlx.mossformer2_se_48k.fp32` loaded from explicit local weight files rather than the HuggingFace cache. Inference is identical; only the load path differs.
